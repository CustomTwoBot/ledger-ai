Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Overview

LedgerAI is a developer tool for tracking and controlling LLM API costs. We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the service. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties except as required to run the service.

What we collect

email
Used to identify your account and deliver your API key. Nothing else.
api_key
A randomly generated token that authenticates your SDK requests.
agent_id
A label you choose to identify which agent made a call. We never inspect its contents.
token counts
Input and output token counts per LLM call — used to calculate cost.
model / provider
Which model was called (e.g. claude-haiku-4-5, gpt-4o).
timestamp
When the call was made — used for trend charts and daily resets.
✕

We do not collect: the content of your prompts or responses, passwords, payment information, IP addresses, or any data beyond what is listed above.

How we use it

  • Authenticate your SDK requests via API key
  • Calculate and display your LLM spending on the dashboard
  • Enforce budget limits and trigger alerts you configure
  • Generate cost trends and summaries for your agents

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating LedgerAI.

Where it's stored

  • PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway (US West region)
  • Data is encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • No data is stored on your local machine by LedgerAI

How long we keep it

  • Your account data is retained while your account is active
  • Cost logs are retained for 90 days by default
  • You can request full deletion at any time — see contact below

Third parties

  • Railway — hosting and database infrastructure

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third party.

Your rights

  • Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • GDPR users: you have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below. We respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of LedgerAI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
